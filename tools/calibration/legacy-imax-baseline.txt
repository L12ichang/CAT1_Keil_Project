- 0x07 Imax: actual Level200/100% output current measured before per-device full-scale gain; it may be below or above SET_OUTCUR
- firmware full-scale gain: derive internally from calibrated target SET_OUTCUR / pre-gain 0x07 Imax; host never searches or writes PWM
- after 0x07: formal Level 0,20,...,200 sweep uses the internal full-scale gain
- 11-point full-scale acceptance: final Standard Iout must land near configured SET_OUTCUR, not near the pre-gain 0x07 Imax
- 11-point PWL: corrects residual/nonlinear output mapping and measurement mapping after full-scale gain correction
- SET_OUTCUR: product/runtime rated-current target and the calibrated full-scale target for this calibration; it is not Hardware Max
- HWMAX / I-V / absolute-fail limits remain independent physical safety envelopes
